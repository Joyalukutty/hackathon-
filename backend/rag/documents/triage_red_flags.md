# Emergency Triage Red Flags (Synthetic Demo)

## Emergency Severity Index (ESI) Level 1 Criteria
Patients requiring immediate life-saving intervention.
- Cardiac or respiratory arrest.
- SpO2 < 90% despite supplemental oxygen.
- Unresponsive patient (GCS < 9).
- Severe respiratory distress with agonal breathing.
- Anaphylactic shock.
- Profound hypotension (SBP < 70 mmHg) with signs of hypoperfusion.

## Emergency Severity Index (ESI) Level 2 Criteria
High risk situations, confusion/lethargy/disorientation, or severe pain/distress.
- Chest pain suspicious for ischemia.
- Signs of acute stroke (FAST positive, last known well < 4.5 hours).
- Severe sepsis criteria met.
- Extreme pain (e.g., kidney stone, severe burn).
- Suicidal or homicidal ideation with imminent risk.
- New onset confusion in elderly.
