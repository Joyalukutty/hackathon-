# Sepsis Clinical Guideline (Synthetic Demo)

## Overview
This is a synthetic guideline for early detection and management of sepsis in the emergency department setting.

## Red Flags
- Temperature > 38.3°C or < 36.0°C
- Heart Rate > 90 bpm
- Respiratory Rate > 20 breaths/min
- Altered mental status
- Systolic Blood Pressure < 90 mmHg or MAP < 65 mmHg

## Protocol
1. Immediate lactate measurement.
2. Obtain blood cultures prior to administration of antibiotics.
3. Administer broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 hour of recognition.
4. Administer 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L.
5. If hypotensive during or after fluid resuscitation, apply vasopressors to maintain MAP ≥ 65 mmHg.
